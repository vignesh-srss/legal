# Privacy Policy for Lucky Quotes

**Effective Date:** May 03, 2026

At **Lucky Quotes**, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data and information when you use our app.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use
We do not collect any personal information (such as your name, email, or address) from you directly. However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

### Third-Party Service Providers
The app uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. To serve ads, Google may collect:
* **Advertising ID**: A unique, user-resettable ID for advertising.
* **Device Information**: Technical data about your device type and OS version.
* **Location Data**: General location based on your IP address.

For more information, please visit [Google’s Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 2. Local Data Storage
For your convenience, we store the following data locally on your device:
* Your selected language.
* Your list of favorite quotes.
* Onboarding status.

This data is stored using **Room Database** and **DataStore** and never leaves your device. We cannot access this information.

---

## 3. Permissions
* **Camera**: Used only if you choose to take a photo for custom quote backgrounds.
* **Storage**: Used to save generated quote images to your gallery.
* **Internet**: Required to load advertisements.

---

## 4. Advertisements
Advertisements are shown to support the maintenance and development of this app. You can opt-out of personalized advertising in your Android device settings (**Settings > Google > Ads**).

---

## 5. Security
We value your trust. While no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, we use industry-standard libraries to ensure your app experience is safe.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy
These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
