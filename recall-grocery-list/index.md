# Privacy Policy for Recall Grocery List

**Effective Date:** May 07, 2026

Recall Grocery List ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### Local Data Storage
The core functionality of the App is designed to work offline. All grocery lists, items, and categories you create are stored locally on your device using a secure database. We do not upload your lists or personal shopping habits to any external servers.

### Camera and Gallery Permissions
If you choose to add custom items with photos, the App will request access to your device's Camera or Gallery. 
- **Camera:** Used only to take a photo of an item to display in your list.
- **Gallery:** Used only to allow you to select an existing photo of an item.
These images are stored locally on your device and are not shared with us or any third parties.

## 2. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

To keep the App free, we use **Google AdMob** to show advertisements. These third-party vendors may use cookies or device identifiers to serve ads based on your visits to this and other apps.

**Google AdMob may collect:**
- Device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID).
- General location data (IP address).
- App usage statistics and performance data.

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 3. Data Security
We value your trust in providing us your information. Since your data is stored locally, its security depends on the security of your device. We recommend using device-level security (like PIN or Biometrics) to protect your information.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers (if applicable) or local storage.

## 5. Offline Functionality
The App is fully functional without an internet connection. Ads will only be requested and displayed when your device is connected to the internet. 

## 6. Links to Other Sites
This App may contain links to other sites (such as the Privacy Policy link itself or Advertisements). If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
